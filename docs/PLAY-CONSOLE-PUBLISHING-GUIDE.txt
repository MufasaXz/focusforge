FocusForge helps you make time for studying and set thoughtful boundaries
around the apps and feeds that distract you. Plan your time, start a focus
session, and see how your effort grows.

PLAN A DAY OR A WEEK
Choose a focus target from 0 to 12 hours and divide it between your subjects.
Every subject allocation must add up to the target before you save. Daily
plans apply to today; weekly plans repeat with fresh progress each Monday.
Your progress ring appears after you save a target.

FOCUS YOUR WAY
Use Pomodoro presets or create and name your own plans. Manage the presets
you want to keep, choose a clock face, and layer ambient sounds under your
session. Track completed study sessions and view weekly and monthly progress.

SET APP BOUNDARIES
Choose distracting apps to pause, give them daily time budgets, or restrict
them only while focusing. Normal app boundaries use optional Usage Access
and Draw over other apps access. A visible notification lets you stop the
background app-boundaries service. Saved rules stay inactive until the
required access is granted.

USE YOUTUBE WITH INTENTION
Optional supported YouTube Shorts and feed filters use Android Accessibility
access. You see a separate explanation and choose whether to enable it.
Filtering depends on supported YouTube screens and may change after updates.
FocusForge is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google.

SEE YOUR PROGRESS
Build study streaks and earn achievements. Linked accounts back up earned
badge IDs and dates. Optional study groups and leaderboard participation
share selected names and progress summaries; detailed study logs stay on
your device. Add Retro clock, Study session, Daily goal or Weekly progress
widgets to your Android home screen.

MAKE IT YOURS
Choose day, dark or system theme, an accent color and your preferred clock
face. Supported Android and device software may offer live timer surfaces;
availability depends on your device and settings.

STAY IN CONTROL
Study without granting blocking access. Sign in with Google or a verified
email address when you want account features. Sign out keeps your account
and this device's history. Delete account removes associated live account
data and clears progress on this device after successful cleanup.

FocusForge is free and open source in this release, with no ads. Android

8. PERMISSION AND FOREGROUND-SERVICE DECLARATIONS

Manifest requests INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE for Firebase/sign-in;
POST_NOTIFICATIONS for optional timer notices; POST_PROMOTED_NOTIFICATIONS
for supported live surfaces; PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS; SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW;
FOREGROUND_SERVICE and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE. Android also adds
an app-specific receiver permission. No contacts, SMS, location, camera,
microphone or QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES permission is requested by this artifact.
Launcher-app queries power the local app picker; installed-app information
is sensitive and must remain limited to the disclosed functionality.

Usage Access: optional Home card below Start session explains screen-time
totals, daily budgets and detecting selected apps locally. On click, show
the disclosure before Android Settings. Refusal keeps the timer available.
Overlay access: shows the pause screen over a selected normal app. Shield
requests this in context. Rules can be saved pending Usage Access, with
inactive labels; overlay access alone cannot detect the foreground app.

Accessibility declaration: this isAccessibilityTool is FALSE. FocusForge
is a study/productivity app, not a disability support tool. Declare local
supported YouTube Shorts/feed view-ID filtering and its pause screen.
The app does not inspect unrelated app contents, record messages or upload
YouTube screen contents. Package-only checks dismiss a visible pause when
leaving YouTube. Local pause events may be retained for local progress.
Describe the actual disclosure and affirmative consent. Never claim covert
monitoring, Settings automation, uninstall protection or bypassing security.

Suggested Accessibility explanation (review against actual behavior):

Answer YES to collection where Firebase-backed features transmit user data.
Do not select 'no data collected' or 'end-to-end encrypted'. Network transport

Review each Console data category, SDK guidance and the current privacy page.

Data / destination / purpose / optionality:

  SDK device/identifier/security processing under the current form definitions.

Likely form categories to assess: Personal info (email/name/user IDs), App
activity (study summaries/achievements or other user-generated content), and

10. ACCOUNT DELETION AND RETENTION

App path: You > Account & privacy > Delete account. The confirmation warns
that deletion is irreversible and removes profile, achievements and progress.
Fresh password or Google reauthentication comes before cleanup. The client
deletes owned live Firestore records in acknowledged pages/transactions,
including achievements, known stats/preferences/sessions, indexed historical
leaderboard/membership rows and inventoried legacy nested records. Group
ownership transfers or empty groups/invites are removed. Firebase Auth is
deleted LAST. On success local cache/state is cleared and Welcome opens.
Offline/interrupted cleanup may remove some records before failure; the app
keeps local history until success and supports retry. It is not one global
atomic operation and does not automatically finish while the app is closed.

A UID-only accountDeletions fence with request time remains to prevent stale
sessions recreating data. It contains no name/email/progress, has no automatic
TTL, and must be accurately disclosed as a limited security retention.
Other devices' local history is not remotely erased by this client flow.
Sign out retains the account, badge backup and device history. Uninstall alone
does not delete cloud data. Never promise instant erasure of all backups.

Public deletion URL must remain usable without reinstalling the app. The
website email button prepares a draft, not automatic fulfillment. Owner must
monitor the inbox, verify account ownership securely, perform trusted cleanup
for valid external requests and confirm fulfillment/any justified retention.
Do not request passwords, recovery codes or sign-in verification codes.
Firebase's privacy guidance says logged Auth IPs are retained for a few weeks
and other authentication information is removed from live/backups within
180 days after customer-initiated deletion. Review provider terms and explain
app-specific security/support retention. No universal guaranteed turnaround
for developer support requests has been established; publish an honest policy.
