# FocusForge 1.0.7

A stronger offline Pip, cleaner account flows and fixes across Focus and Shield.

- **Offline Pip:** more school lessons, adaptive practice, study planning and
  multi-part answers. Better handling of English typos, Hindi/Hinglish requests
  and follow-up questions. Pip runs on-device without sending questions to AI
  providers. Its movements are smoother and its thoughts appear less often.
- **Focus:** a large dial with Full screen beneath it, the familiar floating
  play/pause bar with separate −5/+5 controls, and safer timer/audio behavior.
- **Your profile:** the familiar compact name/level/XP layout, larger Leaderboard
  and Study Groups tiles, and a new illustrated avatar collection unlocked every
  five levels through level 50. Custom profile photos remain available.
- **Sign-in:** consistent account screens, a live verification resend countdown,
  returning accounts routed to permissions, and fixes that keep a signed-out
  account's identity and achievements separate from a new guest session.
- **Shield:** enabling an app rule now asks for missing Usage Access directly,
  even if app-list access is already allowed. Rules wait for verified permissions.
- **Privacy and security:** stronger account export/deletion controls, bounded
  photo decoding, input validation and other account/backend hardening.
- **GitHub updates:** a dismissible notice for newer published versions/builds,
  plus a manual check in About. Checks run on launch/resume at most once a day
  and wait while a focus session is running. The separate Play Store branch
  remains version 1.0.1 and excludes this updater.

**Build:** 3003. The APK is signed and obfuscated. Install over an existing
compatible GitHub build to keep local data. Google Play uses a different signing
certificate; its updates are delivered through Play. Export local study data
before switching distribution channels.

The update notice starts working after installing 1.0.7; older builds do not
contain it. Pip is an offline rule-based companion, not a newly trained model.
